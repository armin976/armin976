### Hi, I'm Armin 👋

I build **[Noema](https://noemaai.com)**, a private AI app that runs language and vision models right on your iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro. Your models, files, and chats stay on your device unless you choose otherwise.

Most of my work lives in the **[@noemaai-labs](https://github.com/noemaai-labs)** org, not on this profile. Head over there.

#### Where to look

- **[noema](https://github.com/noemaai-labs/noema)**: the open-source community edition of the native Apple app (SwiftUI, llama.cpp, MLX)
- **[noema-atlas](https://github.com/noemaai-labs/noema-atlas)**: a Rust engine for verified, multi-source distribution of local model weights
- **[noema-website](https://github.com/noemaai-labs/noema-website)**: the source for noemaai.com, its docs, model catalog, and Blueprint setup
- **[Noema 1.5 2B](https://noemaai.com/models/noema-1-5-2b)**: our compact Apache 2.0 open-weight model for local use
